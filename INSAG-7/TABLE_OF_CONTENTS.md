## What this document is for

IAEA Safety Series No. 75-INSAG-7 (1992), the **authoritative international account of the Chernobyl Unit 4 disaster** of 26 April 1986, written after the USSR released new information in 1989–1991. It revises INSAG-1: the accident is now attributed to the **positive scram (rod-insertion) design defect combined with an extremely low operating reactivity margin**, not merely operator error. Includes two full Soviet annexes: the SCSSINP Commission report (Annex I) and the USSR experts' working-group report (Annex II), with the accident sequence, RBMK physics, and remedial measures.

**Use it for:**

- Chernobyl accident sequence, minute-by-minute, and root/contributing causes.
- RBMK-1000 design: positive void coefficient, control rod/displacer geometry, ORM, AZ-5 scram behaviour.
- Safety culture and regulatory failure analysis (the origin of the term in nuclear safety).
- Correcting the common INSAG-1-era misconceptions (ECCS blocking, 700 MW limit, operator error claims).
