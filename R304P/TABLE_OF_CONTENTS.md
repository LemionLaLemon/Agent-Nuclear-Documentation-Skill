## What this document is for

USNRC HRTD **Westinghouse Technology Systems Manual** for a **four-loop Westinghouse pressurized water reactor** (the classic 17×17 fuel, RCCA, U-tube steam generator plant). It mirrors R304B in structure and depth for the PWR: reactor coolant system, chemical and volume control, emergency core cooling, containment, rod control, neutron monitoring, pressurizer control, steam generator water level control, reactor protection and ESFAS.

**Use it for:**

- Explaining how a Westinghouse PWR system works end-to-end (RCS, CVCS, RHR/ECCS, AFW, steam dump).
- PWR-specific components: reactor vessel internals, RCCAs, CRDMs, pressurizer, steam generators.
- Control and protection logic: rod control, ARPI/DRPI, ex-core and in-core instrumentation, RPS/ESFAS.
- Comparing PWR and BWR approaches to the same function (ECCS, containment, reactivity control).

> **Note:** Contains two versions of section 2.1 (Reactor Physics Review, rev and base) as supplied.
